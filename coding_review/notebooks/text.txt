Document ID: AM-HEA-001
Topic: Laser Powder Bed Fusion (LPBF) of AlCoCrFeNi High-Entropy Alloys

1. Material Composition
The AlCoCrFeNi system is a transition-metal HEA. For this study, the molar ratios are maintained at Al(0.3)CoCrFeNi. The pre-alloyed powder has a particle size distribution (D50) of 35 μm, optimized for rheological flow in the powder bed.

2. Process Parameters (LPBF)
To achieve a relative density >99.5%, the following laser parameters are utilized:
- Laser Power (P): 200 W
- Scanning Speed (v): 800 mm/s
- Hatch Spacing (h): 0.1 mm
- Layer Thickness (t): 30 μm
- Volumetric Energy Density (VED): E = P / (v * h * t) = 83.3 J/mm³

3. Microstructural Characteristics
The as-printed state exhibits a dual-phase structure consisting of a Face-Centered Cubic (FCC) matrix with BCC/B2 precipitates. Columnar grains are oriented parallel to the building direction (Z-axis) due to the steep thermal gradient (G) and high cooling rate (R), typically exceeding 10^6 K/s.

4. Mechanical Properties
- Yield Strength (σy): 650 MPa
- Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS): 820 MPa
- Elongation at Break: 15%
- Vickers Hardness (HV0.5): 320 HV

5. Post-Processing
Heat treatment at 1100°C for 2 hours followed by water quenching promotes phase homogenization and reduces residual stresses induced by the cyclic thermal expansion and contraction during the LPBF process.
